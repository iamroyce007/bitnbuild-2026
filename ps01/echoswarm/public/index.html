EchoSwarm Collapse-site survivor detection

Every phone on site becomes an ear under the rubble.

Rescuers drop phones on the debris. Each one listens for knocks, voices and vibration. Command gets one live map of where people are most likely trapped.

Open command console
01

Drop

Any phone joins the swarm by scanning a QR code. No special hardware to buy or ship.

02

Self-map

The phones chirp to each other and work out their own positions. No GPS needed inside a collapse.

03

Listen

Each phone detects sounds on the device. Its accelerometer tells knocks travelling through the rubble apart from crew noise.

04

Confirm

The nearest phone calls out in Tamil, Hindi or English and listens for a knock back, confirming the person is conscious.
